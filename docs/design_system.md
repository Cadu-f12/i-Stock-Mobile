# I-STOCK Design System — Version 4

**Coverage:** I-STOCK mobile and web interfaces  
**Functional basis:** mobile requirements v6  
**Date:** November 14, 2026

This document covers interface design; it does not specify architecture, database, API, or the technical implementation of synchronization.

## 1. Scope and permissions

On mobile, I-STOCK is a simplified tool for consulting cutting inserts and updating balances through entries and withdrawals. Master data is read-only. Every recorded movement appears in the history, which is also read-only.

| Information / area on mobile | Consult | Record movement | Create/edit/remove master data | Edit/remove history |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Cutting inserts and balances | Yes | Entry and withdrawal | No | Not applicable |
| Movement history | Yes | Generated by entry/withdrawal | No | No |
| Users, manufacturers, suppliers, reasons | Yes | Not applicable | No | Not applicable |
| Cutting insert photos | View/capture local image | Associate with cutting insert | Not applicable | Remove/replace local photo |

Maintaining cutting inserts and other master data is not allowed on mobile. The web interface may have its own maintenance rules, defined by the web system requirements; mobile permission limits must not be generalized to the web.

### Typography, spacing, and shape

- Inter; alternatives: Segoe UI, Roboto, sans-serif.
- Page title: 26–30 px, weight 700; section: 17–19 px; body: 14 px; metadata: minimum 12 px.
- Quantities use tabular figures.
- 4 px scale: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 40.
- Buttons and fields use an 8 px radius; cards use a 12 px radius.
- Soft outlines, clean hierarchy, and strong contrast for industrial use.
- Touch targets of at least 44 × 44 px.

## 3. Navigation and hierarchy

Primary navigation is fixed at the bottom, as a task bar with icon and label. The home screen is **Stock**, with search and a cutting insert list; do not open on a metrics dashboard.

Mobile destinations:

1. **Stock** — cutting insert list and balances.
2. **History** — read-only movements.
3. **Data** — read-only auxiliary information.
4. **Photos** — capture and view local photos associated with cutting inserts.
5. **Synchronization** — connection, pending items, failures, and conflicts.

Local notifications are accessible through an indicator/alert center at the top, without occupying a primary bottom-bar destination. The active page combines DDA blue, a soft background, and an accessible state. On the web, preserve names and hierarchy; adapt width, navigation, and layout without hiding important states.

## 4. Screen and component patterns

### Stock — main screen

- Search by SKU, description, and manufacturer; filter by status.
- Each item shows SKU, description, manufacturer, minimum stock, balance, data update time, and status.
- Balances consulted from the device must be identified as local and show the last known update.
- When there are pending items, show the projected balance including local entries and withdrawals not yet confirmed. Distinguish cached, projected, and API-confirmed balances when they differ.
- Show a low-stock state when the projected balance is less than or equal to the minimum; show an out-of-stock state when no quantity is available for withdrawal.
- Visible actions: consult item and record entry/withdrawal. Do not show create, edit, or remove master-data actions on mobile.

### Movement recording

- Choose a cutting insert and type: entry or withdrawal.
- Show the local/projected balance before recording; validate a positive integer quantity.
- A withdrawal may not exceed the available balance calculated from the cached balance and all local pending movements.
- Show reason, responsible user, and date/time in the confirmation summary.
- Confirm with an explicit action. A quantity error appears beside the field and reports the available balance.
- After an online recording, state “Confirmed”. Offline, state “Pending submission on this device”.
- Do not allow editing or removing movements that have already been recorded.

### History

Show type, cutting insert, quantity, reason, responsible user, date/time, and synchronization status. The list is read-only and supports search/filter. Use clear states for confirmed, pending, failed, and conflict. Entries and withdrawals have text and an icon in addition to color.

### Auxiliary data

Users, manufacturers, suppliers, reasons, and other available data are read-only on mobile. Do not show buttons for creating, editing, removing, or actions that suggest maintenance. If a record is inactive, indicate its status without deleting historical relationships.

### Offline and synchronization

- Prioritize local information and show “Local data” and “Updated on [date/time]”.
- If required data is not on the device, explain that it is unavailable offline; do not simulate current information.
- Show the number of pending movements and allow the user to track each one's status.
- Failures retain pending items intact and show an option to try again.
- When a change made in the web system causes a conflict, say that the local operation was preserved, distinguish the local balance from the confirmed balance, and avoid silently overwriting data. Final confirmation follows the server response.
- Do not present a pending operation as confirmed.

### Photos and camera

Capture may be started from the cutting insert detail and the Photos page. Select the cutting insert before opening the camera; offer preview, retake, cancel, and associate with the cutting insert. The page states that the image remains local to the device and is not sent to the API or synchronized with other devices. Handle camera denial/unavailability, missing photos, or inaccessible files without blocking consultation of the cutting insert.

### Local notifications

Provide alerts for pending movements and low stock. If data is out of date, state that in the alert. Use a title, affected cutting insert or quantity, reason for the alert, and access to the related area. Timing, frequency, and preferences are not yet defined; do not assume settings or frequency in the visual default.

## 5. States and content

All main screens cover loading, content, empty, and error states. The interface also distinguishes:

- Stock updated by the API.
- Local cached data, with the last update date.
- Projected balance with pending items.
- Confirmed, pending, failed, or conflicted movement.
- Required information unavailable offline.
- Camera access unavailable and photo unavailable.
- Low stock, out of stock, and alerts based on out-of-date data.

Messages must say what happened and the next step. Example: “No connection. The withdrawal was stored on this device and is awaiting synchronization.” For a conflict: “The system balance changed. Your local movement remains preserved and is awaiting confirmation.”

## 6. Mobile and web consistency

- Reuse palette, typography, naming, states, and hierarchy across platforms.
- On mobile, prioritize touch, bottom navigation, cards, and a single form column.
- On the web, use the available width; tables are appropriate when they keep data comparable. Adapt the same cards on narrow screens.
- Preserve SKU, balance, minimum, update time, status, and synchronization status at all sizes.
- Hover must never be the only way to discover an action.
- The permission matrix described here is specific to mobile; web controls follow that product's own requirements.

## 7. Accessibility

- Target WCAG AA contrast; visible keyboard focus; logical navigation sequence.
- Bottom bar with named icons and visible labels; identify the current page semantically.
- Associate errors with fields; announce quantities with units.
- States must never depend only on color, position, or animation.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences and do not use animation as the only synchronization indicator.
